5.0Certification

The media described in section 1.0 was presented in a failed mechanical state and processed under the F.R.O.S.T. procedure. Recovery was performed read-only throughout. No write operation of any kind was issued to the source media.

20,540 files totalling 105.2 GiB were recovered and verified against the source by file count and byte length. Two files required reconstruction and are itemised in section 4.0. A total of 114,688 bytes across three regions proved unrecoverable, being 0.0001 percent of the data presented. The source media was subsequently ejected and retired.

No further recovery is achievable from the media in its present condition.
